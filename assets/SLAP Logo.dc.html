Logo sketches — SLAP Baby Designs

A bear's head, a stitch, four letters.

Drawn as vectors, so it scales to a shop sign or a swing tag. The mark is a bear face built from circles with a dashed seam across it — the patchwork idea that runs through the bears themselves.

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of one ear. Smallest use is 24 px for the badge and 120 px wide for the horizontal lockup. Baloo 2 at weight 800 for SLAP, 600 for Baby Designs, Karla 600 in wide letterspacing for the tagline.
